Privacy Policy

Marriage Hisaab · Last updated 21 November 2026

  • Stored locally Players, scores, settings and history stay on your device.

Overview

Marriage Hisaab is an offline score calculator for the Marriage card game. The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal information.

Information stored by the app

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Player names
  • Game settings and round results
  • Active game progress and completed game history

This information is stored using the device's local application storage and is never sent to a developer server or shared through the app.

Permissions and access

The app does not require an account. It does not access or collect information from your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Data deletion

You can clear completed game history from the app. Uninstalling the app removes the app's locally saved game data and settings from the device.

Children's privacy

Marriage Hisaab does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app stores game data only on the user's device, no child personal data is submitted to the developer.

Third-party services

The app does not use advertising, analytics or account services. This privacy policy is hosted using GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary hosting and request information according to its own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its privacy practices change. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Privacy Policy for Marriage Hisaab

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Marriage Hisaab does not collect, store on external servers, or share any personal information.

All data you enter (player names, points, settings and game history) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

The app does not require an account, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

If you uninstall the app, your saved data on the device will be removed.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children.
